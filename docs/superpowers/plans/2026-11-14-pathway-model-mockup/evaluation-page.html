Support to offer

  • Offer a patient navigator to keep the next steps on time.
  • Check that every treatment option was discussed: less surgery is offered after basic education (Zai 2025).
  • Direct imaging referral from the health centre shortens the time to the trigger (a system lever).

Education is not used to lower the survival figures.

Read with care

  • Survival is counted from diagnosis, so an earlier diagnosis looks better even if death comes no later.
  • Sicker patients are often treated faster, which hides part of the effect of waiting.
  • Nordic baseline, Finnish education gradient, American and English delay effects. Not validated on Finnish patients.
