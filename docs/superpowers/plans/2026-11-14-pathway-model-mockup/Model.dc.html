Trigger criteria, lung

Suspicion → imaging · Sweden
  • New airway symptoms for 6 weeks, smoker or ex-smoker over 40
  • Chest or shoulder pain without another explanation
  • Haemoptysis
Well-founded suspicion → pathway · Norway, Sweden
  • Imaging finding suggesting lung cancer (both)
  • Metastasis or biopsy elsewhere suggesting lung origin (both)
  • Repeated haemoptysis, even with a normal X-ray (Sweden)
  • Superior vena cava obstruction (Sweden)
  • Recurrent laryngeal nerve palsy (Sweden)

Helsedirektoratet, pakkeforløp lungekreft (3 July 2026); RCC, SVF lungcancer v1.4 (2024-11-05). Denmark to load. Finland has no national criteria.

Two settings, shown as a range

  • Finland residual (default on): the part of the Finnish shortfall that timing and education do not explain, as one factor per site and sex.
  • Days before the trigger count (default off): Sud's per-day rate on the days lost before imaging. An assumption; part of it is lead time.
